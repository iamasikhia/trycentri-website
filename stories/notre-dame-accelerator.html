Centri AI joins Notre Dame's Race to Revenue accelerator

A place in the IDEA Center's 2026 cohort, and over $6,000 awarded to help small businesses with AI integration and enablement.

This summer, Centri AI was selected for Race to Revenue (R2R), the University of Notre Dame IDEA Center's accelerator for its most promising student founders, and in July 2026 was awarded over $6,000 to support our work helping small businesses with AI integration and enablement.

Now in its eighth year, R2R is a 10-week residential program where founders build their ventures full-time from the IDEA Center's offices on campus. The 2026 cohort brought together 12 student founders, supported by 16 dedicated interns.

A summer of building

  • Customer discovery and revenue: sharpening the business model with real customers.
  • Mentors and coaches: personalised guidance on everything from go-to-market strategy to pitch refinement.
  • Guest speakers and site visits: learning first-hand from founders of established local businesses.
  • Demo Day at Chicago Tech Week: the summer closed with the cohort presenting to alumni, venture capitalists and industry leaders.
“We build founders, not companies.”
John Henry, Director of Student Startups, IDEA Center

A proven program

Since R2R launched in 2019, 33 of its companies have reached six figures in revenue or funding, eight have passed $1 million, and two have passed $10 million. Over the last four years, more than half of R2R founders have earned or raised at least $100,000, according to the IDEA Center.

What it means for Centri AI

The award goes directly into our mission: reaching more small service businesses, building practical AI integrations around their day-to-day operations, and running hands-on enablement so their teams keep using the tools long after setup.
