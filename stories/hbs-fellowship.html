Milestone · July cohort

Selected for the Harvard Business School Foundry AI Fellowship

Recognition for hands-on work helping service businesses build enterprise-grade AI agents.

Centri AI founder Paul Asikhia has been selected for the Harvard Business School Deep Tech Fellowship (AI Track), July cohort.

The selection follows months of direct work with service-based small businesses that need technical help building enterprise-grade AI agents. As part of his master's project, Paul has worked with these businesses end to end: running discovery calls, integrating agentic systems into the tools they already use, and guiding AI enablement through hands-on training, all using Centri's structured playbook.

“It has been an eye-opening experience.”

The work behind it

  • Discovery: understanding how each business actually runs, and where time is being lost.
  • Integration: building agentic systems that work inside the software the team already uses.
  • Enablement: hands-on training so the team can use, question and improve what's been built.

The fellowship strengthens that same mission: bringing the kind of AI capability usually reserved for large enterprises to the small businesses that keep their communities running.
