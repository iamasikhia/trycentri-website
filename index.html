II.

Empowering teams and agents with Business Brains

Context is King. The more your AI knows about your business, the better its work. We connect the tools your team already uses into one shared brain, so every person and every AI agent works from the same up-to-date picture.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Email, calendar, CRM, books and team chat, linked securely in one place, with your permissions intact.

  2. 02

    Remember

    A self-updating memory of clients, jobs, quotes and decisions. Context that grows as the work happens.

  3. 03

    Act

    AI agents that follow up leads, prepare quotes and keep everyone in sync, with your team approving what matters.

VII.

Common questions

We're a small team. Is this really for us?

Yes, it's who we work with most. Small teams feel the time savings fastest, because every hour returned goes straight back to clients or to the owner's evening.

Do we need to be good with technology?

No. If your team can use email and a calendar, they can use what we set up. Teaching is half of what we do.

What about client privacy and our data?

We choose tools and settings that keep your data private, write clear rules for what should and shouldn't go into AI, and document everything we configure.

Will we have to switch software?

Almost never. We work inside the tools you already pay for, and often find subscriptions you can cancel.

What does it cost?

Projects are scoped and priced up front after a free consultation, with an optional monthly plan for ongoing support. No long contracts.
